About

Background

A brief introduction

Nicholas Chin Bo You (陈柏佑) — Nicholas is my English given name, Bo You my Chinese given name, and Chin my family name. Born and raised in Malaysia, I graduated from Foon Yew High School JB in Johor Bahru in 2023. I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Physics with an emphasis in Computational Physics and Astronomy, alongside a second major in Applied Mathematics, at the University of Utah.

I work as an undergraduate research assistant under Prof. Tugdual LeBohec and Prof. David Kieda, analyzing observational data from Stellar Intensity Interferometry at the VERITAS telescope array. In addition to research, I serve as the Chair of the Undergraduate Student Advisory Council (USAC) of the Department of Physics & Astronomy at University of Utah. I am also building a SatNOGs-compatible ground station with UCubesat, where I serve as Chief Financial Officer and Ground Station Subsystem Team Lead. Additionally, I also serve as Treasurer for the Fintech Club, making sure we have pizzas for attendees every week!

Outside of academics, I play violin in the Campus Symphony, plays badminton with friends during the weekend, and occasionally go rock climbing.

Current Focus Areas

Stellar Intensity Interferometry

Analyzing SII observational data from the VERITAS array to resolve photosphere pulsation modes of variable stars.

Quantum Information Science & Algorithms

Studying Efficient Quantum State Preparation, QPIE, and quantum advantage — with an eye toward near-term applications on NISQ devices.

Quantitative Finance

Exploring market microstructure, stochastic modeling, and Monte Carlo methods — connecting ideas from Game Theory to Finance.
