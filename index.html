About

Nicholas Chin Bo You (陈柏佑) -- Nicholas is my English given name, Bo You my Chinese given name and Chin as my family name. In some contexts, my name may appear as Nicholas Bo You Chin.

Born and raised in Malaysia, I graduated from Foon Yew High School JB, a Chinese independent high school in Johor Bahru, Malaysia in 2023. During high school, I was deeply involved in the music scene. My experiences ranged from performing as young Schubert for "Sing with Schubert!" Musical Drama at HuzzaMusic in 2019, to participating in both local and school orchestras. Beyond performances, I studied luthiery under Steve Ng's tutelage and interned at Goldstone Music Recording Studio, where I gained foundational skills in music arrangement and audio recording.

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Physics with emphasis in Computational Physics and Astronomy, along with a minor in Mathematics, at University of Utah. I am also currently working as an undergraduate research assistant under Professor Tugdual LeBohec and Professor David Kieda, focusing on analyzing observational data from Stellar Intensity Interferometry at VERITAS.

In addition to research, I serve as the Chair of the Undergraduate Student Advisory Council (USAC) of the Department of Physics & Astronomy at University of Utah. Outside of academics, I am building a SatNOGS-compatible ground station with UCubesat. continue to play violin in the Campus Symphony, and have been rock climbing for some time.
